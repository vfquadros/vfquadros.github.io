Hidden in plain sight: a model of spatial concealment

Writing sample

Paper Slides

Can illegal firms exploit the infrastructure of legal markets? Data on legal timber shipments suggest that some producers use the market's own regulatory system to conceal illegal timber flows. I show evidence of this mechanism and develop a model in which firms choose whether to operate in the legal or illegal market.
